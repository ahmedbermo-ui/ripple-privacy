Ripple – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

In short: Ripple works entirely on your phone. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access, so it cannot send anything about you anywhere. We (the developer) never receive any of your data.

Who we are

Ripple is an Android app that turns your phone's vibration (or a connected game controller's) into patterns for relaxation and massage. It is published by Osli software ("we"). Contact details are at the end of this policy.

Data we collect

None. The app does not have permission to use the internet, and it contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your phone

To work, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • Patterns you record, your playlists, favourites and recently played patterns.
  • Your settings, such as language, colour style, power level, playback and Music mode preferences.
  • That you confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • If you turn on the calculator disguise: your code, stored only as a one-way scrambled value (a salted hash) in Android's protected storage. The code itself is never saved.

This information is not included in Android's automatic Google Drive backup or device-to-device transfer. It is removed when you use Settings → Erase everything or uninstall the app.

Microphone (Music mode)

Music mode lets the vibration follow music playing near your phone. It uses the microphone only while you have Music mode switched on, and only after you allow it in the Android permission prompt.

  • The app measures how loud the sound is, about 20 times per second, and turns that number into vibration strength.
  • Sound is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Each loudness value is used once and discarded.
  • Listening stops when you tap Stop, switch away from Music mode, or leave the app.

You can refuse or withdraw microphone access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Ripple → Permissions. Everything except Music mode keeps working.

Vibration and game controllers

The app uses your phone's vibration motor, or the rumble of a game controller you connect through Android. It reads only the controller's name to show it on screen; nothing about the controller is stored or sent.

Sharing and backups

You can share a pattern or playlist, or save a backup of your library, as a file. This only happens when you choose to, through Android's share menu, and goes to the app or place you pick (for example WhatsApp or your Files). From then on, that app's own privacy policy applies. A shared file contains the pattern data and the names you gave it; a backup contains your patterns, playlists and favourites. Neither contains your settings or disguise code.

When you open a Ripple file someone sent you, the app reads it only to add the pattern or playlist to your library, after you confirm.

Age requirement

Ripple is intended for adults aged 18 and over and asks you to confirm your age when you first open it. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or hold any personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on our side. All your data is under your control on your device: you can delete it at any time with Settings → Erase everything or by uninstalling the app. Laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK), LGPD (Brazil) and CCPA (California) give you rights over personal data held by companies; since we hold none, please contact us if you have any question about this.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled (for example, by adding optional online features), we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released, and the app will ask for any new permission it needs.
